Who we are

readark OÜ is a company registered in Estonia (registry code [REGISTRY CODE]), with its registered office in Tallinn. We are the controller for any personal data processed through this website.

What this website collects

Nothing by design. readark.eu is a static website. It sets no cookies, loads no analytics or advertising scripts, and contains no forms. The only third-party request the page makes is to load its typefaces from Google Fonts; Google may record your IP address as part of serving that request, under Google's own privacy policy.

Our hosting provider records standard server access logs (IP address, time, requested page, browser type) for security and operations. These logs are not linked to you as a person and are deleted within 30 days.

Email

If you write to hello@readark.eu, we keep your message and address for as long as it takes to deal with your request and for any follow-up you would reasonably expect. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not share your details with anyone.

Products

Each readark product has its own privacy notice, published with the product. Where a product processes personal data, that notice explains what, why and for how long.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you may ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to hello@readark.eu. You may also lodge a complaint with the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

Changes

If this website ever starts collecting data, we will update this page before it does and note the change here.
