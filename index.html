01 — Who we are

A way of seeing things, made real through engineering.

readark begins with an idea of how a thing should be. Every product we make is the closest we can bring that idea into the physical world: the board, the software and the experience, engineered as one.

We choose problems where the distance between what exists and what should exist is wide, and we close that distance ourselves, from the first schematic to the user manual.

We are building a focused portfolio of products, each of which has to earn its place by being the definitive version of what it does. readark is the company. Every product carries its own name and is held to the same standard.

02 — What we work on

Three markets. One discipline.

Our products span sectors that, on the surface, have little to do with each other. The common thread is the engineering underneath: a real-time core, hardware that behaves in the real world, and an interface that hides the complexity from the person using it.

  • A

    Industrial & lab instruments

    Benchtop equipment that has to be accurate, repeatable and quiet enough to live next to its operator.

  • B

    Automotive aftermarket

    Devices that live on a vehicle for years. Automotive-grade parts, sealed enclosures, and hardware interlocks that hold while the software sleeps.

  • C

    Venue & hospitality systems

    Software and a player that run all evening in a busy room, built for the staff at the till as much as for the guest at the table.

03 — How we engineer

Four rules we do not trade away.

  • 01

    Plug in. It works.

    Every readark product does its job on its own, out of the box, with the device in front of you as the only thing you need. Wireless adds to it.

  • 02

    Robustness before features.

    A feature earns its place on the datasheet by holding up in the hands of someone who has never opened the manual. Everything we ship has cleared that bar.

  • 03

    Every number is traceable.

    Specs, tolerances, lifetime figures: if it is on our page or in our manual, it traces back to a measurement, a datasheet or a published study.

  • 04

    Engineering writes the manual.

    The people who designed the software, the board and the enclosure write the user manual and the spec page. What you read is what the engineers decided, in their own words.
