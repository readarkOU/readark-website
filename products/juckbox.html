JuckBox

In development

The jukebox, rebuilt for the venue's own speakers and the guest's own phone.

CategoryHospitality
Core disciplineVenue player + real-time queue
Guest sideScan a code, use the browser
MarketCafés, bars, restaurants
Summary

Scan. Pick a track. Hear it.

What it is

JuckBox puts the venue's music in the guests' hands without putting anything on their phones. A guest scans the code on the table, a web page opens, they search for a track and request it. A player device at the venue holds the queue and plays through the existing sound system. Requests line up in order; paid priority requests move to the front. The staff see and manage the queue from the till.

Why it exists

People used to drop a coin in a jukebox and hear their song. That experience disappeared when music moved to a playlist behind the bar. JuckBox brings it back with the friction removed: no app to install, no account to create, no hardware the guest has to touch. For the venue, it is a new line of revenue that runs on a small box and a QR code.

How it reflects readark

The venue player is a device that has to run all evening, every evening, and recover on its own when the network blinks. The guest experience is one screen with one job. The complexity, from licensing to payments to the live queue, is handled behind it.

Piloting with selected venues

JuckBox is in development and being piloted. If you run a venue and want to be among the first, get in touch.
