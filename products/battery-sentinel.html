Battery Sentinel

In development

A smart battery disconnect for vehicles that have to start the first time.

CategoryAutomotive aftermarket
Core disciplinePower switching & monitoring
ConnectivityBLE for owners, LoRa for fleets
MarketOwners and fleet operators
Summary

Isolation that does not depend on a radio.

What it is

Battery Sentinel sits between the battery and the vehicle. It watches the battery's state, isolates it mechanically before a parasitic drain can flatten it, and reconnects when the owner needs the car. A Bluetooth app gives the owner status and control; a LoRa link gives fleet operators monitoring across a lot full of vehicles that are rarely driven.

Why it exists

Vehicles that sit for weeks are the ones that fail to start: seasonal cars, dealer stock, rental and utility fleets. Existing disconnects are either a manual switch or an app-dependent gadget. Battery Sentinel is designed so that the isolation itself never depends on the radios, the phone or a server being available.

The idea behind it

The ideal battery guard is one the owner forgets exists, because the vehicle always starts. Battery Sentinel is built toward that idea: the decision to isolate is made on the device, by the device, and holds whether or not a phone, a radio or a server is reachable. Wireless makes it more useful; it never becomes the condition for it to work.
