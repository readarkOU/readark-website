Summary

What it is

AnnealX is a benchtop oven engineered for one job: bringing a 3D-printed part to a target temperature, holding it there, and cooling it in a controlled way. The user selects a material on the touchscreen and starts the run. The device tracks the full thermal profile, from a short PLA cycle to a day-long high-performance polymer soak, without supervision.

Why it exists

Annealing is the single most effective post-process for making printed parts stronger and dimensionally stable, and it is usually done in kitchen ovens with guesswork. AnnealX replaces the guesswork with measured recipes. Every material profile shipped on the device traces back to a peer-reviewed paper, a national-lab study or a manufacturer datasheet, and the source is shown on the device.

The idea behind it

The ideal annealer has one job and does it exactly: it brings a part to the right temperature, holds it there, and cools it the way the polymer needs. Nothing else stands between the user and that outcome. AnnealX is our closest approach to that idea. It works fully offline, every recipe on it traces back to a source, and the engineers who designed the controller wrote the manual. Every number on the product page is a measurement.
